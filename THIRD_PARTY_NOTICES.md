# Third-party components

The project does not currently include a project-wide license for its original source. This does not replace or alter the licenses of third-party components.

- The Franka Emika Panda model is supplied by [MuJoCo Menagerie](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_menagerie) through the pinned submodule. Its upstream [Panda license](mujoco_menagerie/franka_emika_panda/LICENSE) and Menagerie notices remain with the model.
- Python packages listed in `requirements-agent.txt` and `requirements-planning.txt` are installed separately and retain their respective upstream licenses.

The repository contains no model-service credentials or downloaded VLM weights.
